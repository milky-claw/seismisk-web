How the round trip works

Where the text goes: your server, local weights, no outside call. Only tokens leave.

  1. 1

    Redact

    Names, addresses and account numbers become typed tokens. Local weights, no outside call.

  2. 2

    Register

    Same name, same token. The registry stays on that machine and is the only way back.

  3. 3

    Send tokens

    ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok gets [PERSON_1] at [ADDRESS_1] asking about [REDACTED_1]. It drafts in tokens.

  4. 4

    Rehydrate

    The reply returns. The registry puts the originals back. Unknown tokens are refused, not guessed.

What the law says

Not a ban. A disclosure, allowed only under conditions.

GDPR

Art. 28 processor contract; Chapter V safeguards outside the EU; stricter for health data.

Without it: An unlawful transfer.

FERPA

Only to a vendor that qualifies as a “school official” under the school's direct control.

Without it: An unconsented disclosure.

COPPA

Under-13 data needs verifiable parental consent that names the recipient.

Without it: No consent for that recipient.

CCPA/CPRA

Only to a service provider under a contract that restricts its use.

Without it: A disclosure outside the contract.
